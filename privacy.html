📋 Why Shift Schedule?
Everything your team needs in one app
📅 Schedule Management

Build and manage the weekly shift schedule for your entire team. Drag shifts to swap, assign First / Second / Third shifts, and see a live breakdown. Changes sync instantly.

👥 Team Management

Add staff and sub-managers from Settings. Set default shifts, expected hours, and passwords. Share your Business Code so new joiners can log in immediately.

📋 Requests Inbox

Leave requests, shift changes and password resets land in your Requests tab. Approve or reject with one tap. Staff get instant notifications.

📊 Status & Attendance

See who is online, track clock-in and clock-out times. Export attendance as PDF or Excel for payroll.

🏖️ Leave Requests

Submit leave from My Stuff — choose Sick, Holiday or Personal. Your manager reviews and you get notified of the decision.

📬 Alerts & Notifications

Receive announcements from your manager in your alerts inbox. A badge shows unread messages.
